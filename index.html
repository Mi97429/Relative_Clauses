Relative Clauses: Rules 🧠

✖
1. Which word do I use?
Refers to Subject Object Possession
People 🧑‍🦱 WHO
"The man who called."		 WHO / WHOM
"The girl whom I saw."		 WHOSE
"The boy whose bike..."
Things 📦 WHICH
"The car which is red."		 WHICH
"The book which I read."		 WHOSE
"A house whose roof..."
Place / Time 🌍 WHERE (Place)
"The city where I live."
WHEN (Time)
WHY (Reason)
2. Comma or not?
DEFINING (No commas)

Information is necessary to identify who/what we are talking about.

"The man who lives next door is nice."

(Without the clause, we don't know which man.)

NON-DEFINING (Commas)

Information is extra. We already know who it is (e.g. a name).

"My father, who is 50, is a doctor."

(I only have one father, age is extra info.)

3. Golden Rules 🏆
  • 🚫 Never use THAT after a comma.
  • 🚫 Never use THAT after a preposition (in, at, with...).
  • 👉 After a preposition, use WHOM (people) or WHICH (things).
    Ex: "The man with whom I work."
  • 👻 If the word (who/which/that) is NOT the subject, you can omit it (in Defining sentences).
    Ex: "The book (which) I read."
